### How to extend

1. **Get a legitimate copy.** Sources are a library, publisher access or the author.
2. **Name the file like the index.** Use `<ID>-<year>-<first eight title words, lowercased, hyphenated>.pdf`, the stem `acquire_fulltexts.py` uses (the ID is the one in INDEX.md).
3. **Put it in `<researcher>/references/sources/papers/`.** This folder's PDFs and `txt/` are git-ignored. Check by hand that the file is the paper, because the title check only runs on downloads.
4. **Re-run the acquire script.** `python3 scripts/acquire_fulltexts.py product/milp-team/<researcher> --only <ID>` extracts the text, OCRs scans, and sets `Full text = txt` and `Source = manual`. For a card marked unreadable because of a garbled text layer (REOCR) use `--reocr <ID>`; for a wrong file (WRONG-TEXT) `--drop <ID>`. Run it in the checkout that holds the other papers' PDFs and `txt/`: rows without local files keep their committed status, but their quotes cannot be re-verified.
5. **Card and fold it in.** Either run `team-increment.js` (T4; see `scripts/workflows/README.md`), which cards the new material, folds it into the skill, verifies and fixes; or by hand: plan a new round with `python3 scripts/plan_reading_batches.py product/milp-team/<researcher> --out-dir <scratch> --no-abstract` (it picks the next round and skips works already read in full or in part or carded unreadable; add `--reread <ID>` for a work re-OCR'd after an unreadable card, or `--round N --exclude <plan files still being read>` to fix the round by hand), read with the brief above, run `verify_card_quotes.py` and `mark_read_from_cards.py`, regenerate `07-paper-cards.md` with `python3 scripts/build_card_index.py <member folder>`, and apply the conservative-update rules to `SKILL.md`, `09-evidence-ledger.md` and `technique-catalog.md`. Open parts of books (the leads in `book-leads.json`) become `B` rows through `team-increment.js`.
6. **Refresh the numbers.** Re-run `python3 scripts/team_status.py product/milp-team --coverage` and paste it unchanged into the Coverage table here; paste the output of `python3 scripts/team_status.py product/milp-team --coverage --short` unchanged over the five-column summary in the team README (or run `team-integrate.js`, which does both).
7. **Guests.** Their reading runs in the LP Team's folder, never here. After the LP Team re-reads or re-synthesizes a guest, rerun `team-integrate.js` here.

## Privacy and integrity

- Private materials stay in each member's `references/sources/private/` (git-ignored; only its README is tracked) and are never read into public files. The deep reading works only from public sources.
- PDFs and extracted text (`papers/*.pdf`, `papers/txt/`) are git-ignored for copyright reasons by the repository's `.gitignore`, and `git ls-files product/milp-team` lists none. Only indexes, abstracts, cards and syntheses are committed.
- Full texts come only from legitimate open copies: arXiv and Optimization Online, Unpaywall, author and coauthor pages (live or as Wayback Machine captures), institutional repositories and report series (ZIB OPUS and its ZIB-Reports, TU Berlin's DepositOnce, GERAD's Les Cahiers, RWTH Publications, DLR elib), Dagstuhl's DROPS and open publisher copies.
- The integrity rules in each skill still apply. No claim goes in without a page reference, and no quote is invented: every quote on a card passes `scripts/verify_card_quotes.py`. Nothing written by a coauthor, reviewer, interviewer or newsletter author is attributed to a member: the cards read the four-author book *Branch-and-Price* as joint voice, the SCIP release reports as group voice, editorials and prefaces as the editors' joint voice, the theses Fischetti supervised as the students', and his 2026 papers with the AI share each declares.
- Sealed validation material: no point, quote or decision of an item sealed in a member's `holdout.md` appears in this record, the team README or the roundtable; the 08 syntheses and ledgers leave out the passages that restate them.
