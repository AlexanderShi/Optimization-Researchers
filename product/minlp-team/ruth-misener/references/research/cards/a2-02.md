### S136 · Online Generation Via Offline Selection of Strong Linear Cuts from a Semidefinite Programming Relaxation (2018 AIChE Annual Meeting, presentation abstract, no DOI)
**Read**: abstract (AIChE programme, Paper 529958, session "Advances in Deterministic Global Optimization") · **Role**: supplement · **Coauthors**: R. Baltean-Lugojan, P. Bonami, A. Tramontani (IBM)

- **D1 (problem/entry)**: SDP relaxations of nonconvex QCQP are tighter than RLT, but in branch-and-cut they lack warm starts and mix badly with linear cuts, so "SDP technology is effectively unavailable" in solvers that use linear relaxations; the open question is when to pay for an SDP cut and over which variables (abstract).
- **D2 (key idea)**: move cut-selection complexity offline: a neural network trained before the solver is installed picks the low-dimensional variable subsets, and linear cuts are then generated from negative eigenvalues (abstract).
- **D5 (experiments)**: several classes of nonconvex engineering problems plus generic QCQP; the strategy is integrated into ANTIGONE (abstract). No numbers in the abstract.
- **D8 (contribution)**: an algorithmic framework that keeps "most tightness" of the SDP relaxation as a few linear cuts; a conference version of the Optimization Online preprint S021 (batch note), so the journal-level evidence sits on S021's card.

**Method links**: Method 4 evidence ✅ (the new cut class is tested inside the full ANTIGONE solver, not only as a stand-alone bound; abstract). New-pattern candidate: "learned offline oracle for an expensive solver decision" (abstract).
**Quotes**: "We seek an inexpensive oracle that will tell us when it is relevant to generate a cut and over which variables." (abstract); "The cut selection complexity is taken offline by using a neural network estimator (trained before installing solver software) as a selection device for the strongest cuts." (abstract)
- Transferable: when a strong relaxation is too expensive to call at every node, train a cheap selector offline (before deployment) that decides where to call it, and use the strong relaxation only to generate linear cuts the existing LP-based solver can consume (abstract).
